Common questions

Are the fragrances original?

Yes. Every bottle is a genuine, brand-original product, sealed and sourced from authorised channels.

How long does delivery take?

Orders are dispatched quickly and sent with tracking to EU destinations. You’ll receive a tracking link by email as soon as your parcel ships.

What is your return policy?

Unopened, sealed items can be returned. For hygiene reasons, opened fragrances can’t be returned unless they arrive damaged or incorrect. Contact us and we’ll sort it out.

Can I get help choosing a gift?

Of course. Use the recommendation guide above or send us a message with the occasion and budget.
